AQA GCSE History (8145) Question Stem & Structure Guide
All 4 Papers • Timings, Assessment Objectives & Paragraph Plans
Paper & Q Question Stem Marks & Time Recommended Paragraph Structure
1A • Q1 How does Interpretation B differ from Interpretation A about...? 4m • 5 mins 2 short paragraphs (AO4 Content only): Summarise Interpretation A’s view, then contrast directly with Interpretation B’s view. Do not discuss provenance.
1A • Q2 Why might the authors of Interpretations A and B have a different interpretation...? 4m • 5 mins 2 short paragraphs (AO4 Provenance + Context): Explain Author A’s background/purpose/date, then Author B’s background/purpose/date using contextual knowledge.
1A • Q3 Which interpretation gives the more convincing opinion about...? 8m • 10 mins 3 paragraphs (AO4 Context Evaluation): Test Interpretation A against historical facts; test Interpretation B against historical facts; conclude which is more convincing. Zero provenance.
1A • Q4 Describe two problems / ways / features... 4m • 5 mins 2 short paragraphs (AO1): One distinct, specific point per paragraph backed by a date, law, or statistic (2 marks each).
1A • Q5 In what ways were the lives of [group] affected by...? 8m • 10 mins 2–3 P-E-E-L paragraphs (AO1/AO2): Analyse different types of impact (economic, social, political) across the period with specific evidence.
1A • Q6 Which of the following was the more important reason why... (2 bullets)? 12m • 15 mins 3 paragraphs (AO1/AO2): Para 1 analyse Bullet 1; Para 2 analyse Bullet 2; Para 3 comparative judgement weighing why one was more important.
1B • Q1 Source A opposes / supports... How do you know? 4m • 5 mins 1–2 paragraphs (AO3 Content + Context): Pick 2 visual or written details from Source A and explain each with exact historical context.
1B • Q2 How useful are Sources A and B to an historian studying...? 12m • 15 mins 3 paragraphs (AO3): Para 1 Source A (Content + Provenance + Context); Para 2 Source B (Content + Provenance + Context); Para 3 combined utility conclusion.
1B • Q3 Write an account of how [event] became an international crisis / led to... 8m • 10 mins 3 chronological stages (AO1/AO2): Trigger → Escalation → Turning Point/Outcome, using causal connectives (consequently, this led to).
1B • Q4 “[Statement].” How far do you agree with this statement? 16m + 4 SPaG • 20m 4 paragraphs (AO1/AO2 + SPaG): Para 1 Stated factor; Para 2 Alternative factor A; Para 3 Alternative factor B; Para 4 Balanced conclusion.
2A • Q1 How useful is Source A to an historian studying...? 8m • 10 mins 2–3 paragraphs (AO3): Evaluate Source A’s content with contextual knowledge, assess Provenance (origin, purpose, date), and note limitations.
2A • Q2 Explain the significance of [person / discovery]... 8m • 10 mins 2 paragraphs (AO1/AO2): Para 1 Short-term impact at the time (and any immediate limits); Para 2 Long-term impact on later medicine.
2A • Q3 Explain two ways in which X and Y were similar / different. 8m • 10 mins 2 comparative paragraphs (AO1/AO2): Inside EACH paragraph, directly compare both time periods with specific examples from each.
2A • Q4 Has [factor] been the main factor in... since c1000? 16m + 4 SPaG • 20m 4 thematic paragraphs (AO1/AO2 + SPaG): Named AQA factor + 2 other AQA factors across Medieval to Modern eras + thematic conclusion.
2B • Q1 How convincing is Interpretation A about...? 8m • 10 mins 3 paragraphs (AO4): Why Interpretation A is convincing using contextual knowledge; where it is limited; overall judgement. Zero provenance.
2B • Q2 & Q3 Explain what was important about... (Q2) / Write an account of... (Q3) 8m • 10 mins Q2: Two developed reasons for historical importance. Q3: Three causal stages from problem to resolution.
2B • Q4 2027 Historic Environment: The Spanish Armada (1588) 16m (No SPaG) • 20m 4 paragraphs (AO1/AO2): Stated Armada factor + 2 alternative Armada factors (e.g., ship design, planning, Cadiz 1587, storms) + site conclusion.
