Paper 2A Q4 Reference

The 7 Official AQA Health & the People Factors

1. War: Paré's ointments (1537), WWI Thomas Splint & Gillies' plastic surgery, WWII penicillin mass production.
2. Superstition & Religion: Medieval Church backing Galen & banning dissection vs. running 1,100 monastic hospitals.
3. Chance: Paré's boiling oil running out, Pasteur's weak chicken cholera, Fleming's 1928 penicillin mould.
4. Government: Jenner's £30k grants, 1875 Public Health Act, 1906–11 Liberal Reforms, 1948 NHS.
5. Communication: Printing press (Vesalius), Royal Society journals, John Snow's 1854 spot map.
6. Science & Technology: Microscopes, Pasteur's Germ Theory (1861), Koch's agar/dyes, X-rays, DNA (1953).
7. Role of the Individual: Hippocrates, Galen, Vesalius, Harvey, Jenner, Pasteur, Koch, Lister, Nightingale, Snow, Bevan.
Paper 2B Q4 • 2027 Specified Site

2027 Historic Environment: The Spanish Armada (1588)

Location & Geography: English Channel, Plymouth Sound, shallow Calais anchorage, North Sea & rocky Irish coast.
Ship Design & Weapons: Hawkins' low, fast race-built galleons & long-range culverins vs. tall Spanish carracks.
Key Commanders: Lord Howard, Drake, Hawkins, Elizabeth I (Tilbury) vs. Philip II, Medina Sidonia, Duke of Parma.
Decisive Tactics: 7 Aug 1588 Calais fireships breaking Spain's crescent; 8 Aug Battle of Gravelines broadsides.
Planning & Logistics: No deep-water Dutch port for Parma's 27,000 troops; Drake's 1587 Cadiz barrel raid.
Weather & Context: September Atlantic storms wrecking ~40 fleeing ships; 1587 execution of Mary, Queen of Scots.
